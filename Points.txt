SIH 26132 — Proposed App Features Strengthening Market Linkages and Price Discovery for Farmers 
1. User Registration & Profiles • Farmer registration and profile • Buyer registration and business profile • Farmer/FPO/buyer verification status • Location and basic farm/business details 
2. Market Intelligence Dashboard • Current mandi and market prices • Nearby market comparison • Historical price trends • Expected price trend/range • Nearby buyer opportunities and alerts 
3. Crop Lot Creation • Create crop lots for selling • Crop, variety, quantity and location details • Harvest date and availability • Quality/grade details • Storage capability and selling preferences • Optional minimum acceptable price 
4. Smart Buyer & Market Matching • Match crop lots with suitable buyers • Compare local mandis, direct buyers, processors and institutional buyers • Match based on crop, quality, quantity, distance and delivery timeline • Show compatible buyer requirements 
5. Net Realisation Engine • Compare actual expected returns instead of only quoted prices • Include transport cost • Include storage/cold-storage cost • Include handling and transaction costs • Include estimated spoilage/loss where applicable • Show net realisation for every selling option 
6. Sell Now vs Wait Recommendation • Compare today's buyer offer with future buyer opportunities• Calculate the cost of waiting • Check storage availability and cost • Recommend Sell Now, Wait, Aggregate or Sell Elsewhere • Explain why an option is recommended 
7. FPO & Farmer Aggregation • Discover and join existing FPOs • FPO membership request and approval workflow • Create temporary farmer selling/aggregation groups • Combine compatible farmer lots • Unlock bulk buyers with minimum quantity requirements • FPO dashboard for member lots and group selling 
8. Buyer Demand Posting • Buyers post crop requirements • Specify quantity, quality, location and delivery date • Specify offer price and payment terms • Set whether the price is negotiable • Notify matching farmers/FPOs 
9. Farmer–Buyer Negotiation • Direct structured offer system • Accept or reject offers • Counter-offers • Negotiate price, quantity and delivery terms • Negotiate pickup responsibility and payment timeline • Maintain negotiation history 
10. Cold Storage & Storage Decisions • Show nearby storage options • Estimate storage cost • Check available storage duration/capacity • Add storage cost automatically to future-sale calculations • Compare waiting benefits against storage and spoilage costs 
11. Logistics Integration • Estimate transport distance and cost • Choose whether farmer, buyer or FPO arranges transport • Support farm-to-buyer and farm-to-storage-to-buyer routes • Basic route planning for aggregated lots • Include logistics cost in net-realisation calculations
12. Buyer Reliability & Trust • Business/identity verification status • Completed transaction history • On-time payment history • Dispute history/rate • Platform-generated reliability indicator 
13. Payment & Transaction Tracking • Clear payment terms before deal confirmation • Track pickup, delivery and quality confirmation • Track payment pending and payment received status • Maintain transparent transaction records 
14. Quality Matching • Buyer-defined quality requirements • Farmer lot quality details • Compatibility check for grade, quantity and other applicable parameters • Optional quality report/assaying document upload 
15. Dispute & Grievance System • Raise a dispute against a transaction • Payment, price, quantity, quality and delivery complaints • Upload supporting evidence • Digital grievance ticket and status tracking • FPO/admin-mediated prototype workflow 
16. Notifications & Alerts • New matching buyer offer • Better net-realisation opportunity • FPO/group aggregation opportunity • Market price changes • Payment reminders • Storage deadline alerts 
17. Transaction History & Analytics • Past crop lots and sales • Past buyers and realised prices • Net-realisation history • Payment and dispute records • Buyer/FPO procurement and matching analytics18. Optional / Future Features • Regional language and voice interface • Advanced price forecasting • Image-based quality assessment • Advanced route optimisation • Government registry/API integrations where authorised • Payment gateway or escrow integration Core USP: Help farmers choose the selling option with the best practical net realisation by considering price, timing, storage, logistics, quantity, quality and buyer reliability.
